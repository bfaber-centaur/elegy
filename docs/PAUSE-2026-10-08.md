## 4. Known bugs, limitations, assumptions and open questions

Each item below is taken from the status document it names. Those
documents hold the full lists.

**Not modelled.**

- The turn: following fleets (#130), waypoint tasks other than those
  listed, the messages for an Orbital Adjuster or remote miner that
  changed nothing, Super Stealth research stealing, the duplicate-serial
  penalty, and the BINARY-ONLY movement rules for IFE, Cheap Engines,
  warp-10 losses and Radiating Hydro-Ram colonist deaths
  (KERNEL-STATUS.md "Not modelled yet").
- Transport tasks:
  - They cannot target fleets, so loads from another player's fleet
    (0x120) and cargo stealing are not modelled. Neither is the "fleet
    has finished its orders" message (0x04e) after a transport task.
  - Every fuel action of a transport task is not modelled.
  - Also not modelled: ancient artifacts, the design check at generation
    for designs made other than by a design order or a computer player,
    and the old owner's message beyond `EventPlanetEmptied`
    (TAKEOVER-STATUS.md "Not modelled").
  - An Alternate Reality colony whose owner has no starbase design gets
    none (UNRESOLVED).
- Reports:
  - Not modelled: remote-mining reports, planet reports after minefield
    hits or a lost planet, design disclosure after SD hits, chase
    retargeting, original environment, artifact and terraformed flags,
    and messages that make a player known (SCANNING-STATUS.md).
- Objects: a manual load from a mineral packet (OBJECTS-STATUS.md).
- Production:
  - Not modelled: the Alternate Reality remote-mining task on a new
    fleet, the "did not move" mark for a fleet built this year, and the
    route task on arrival. Also not modelled: the queued cross-player
    credit routine (UNRESOLVED HYPOTHESIS; ORDERS-LAYER-STATUS.md).
  - Deep-space jettison is refused by Elegy's choice.
- Battles: Mystery Trader items and queued ships and packets lost with a
  starbase (COMBAT-STATUS.md).
- New games: unseeded wizard games, a random computer type or level in a
  definition file, the tutorial galaxy, logos and the original's random
  stream (UNIVERSE-STATUS.md).

**Computer players, held items** (AI-STATUS.md):

- A7: an armed Meta Morph with 500 kT of cargo is not recognised as a
  transport. This needs the design-power formula.
- A23: Cybertron's weak-armada branches are not built, because under
  clean per-player state no armada is ever weak. They come with the
  legacy switch `legacy_ai_state_leak`, which is not implemented.
- A41: hub freighters' pickup marks, small foreign colonies, salvage and
  colonist rules are not built.
- Waypoints to a wormhole end: the engine cannot target one yet, so the
  planners aim at the point where the end was last seen (A46).
- Other players' PRT: the report withholds it, so every other player
  counts as not Alternate Reality.
- Rototill's unreachable branches are reported as unsupported.
  - The long-game regression counts unsupported computer steps as an
    observation, not a failure.

## Retained decisions

- Elegy is the canonical, deterministic, headless engine.
- The original executable is an oracle, not a dependency.
- Research and the private apparatus stay separate from the
  implementation.
- Only Robotoid, Rototill and Cybertron are computer-player targets.
- Ruleset differences stay explicit, as named settings of a per-game
  ruleset (RULESET.md).
- Player views stay separate from the authoritative game state.
- No front end, multiplayer or narrative work is authorised.
