# Takeover status

Bombing, transport unloads, colonist drops, ground combat, colonization
and capture in `engine/takeover.go` follow the public specification
stars-elegy `docs/TAKEOVER.md` on `main` at `8ddc64a`, which includes the
implementer answers merged in PR #34 (order inside a phase, unload
amounts and foreign minerals, capture tech, the mines' loss clamp) and
the corrections merged in PR #37 (deep-space unloads; colonize is tried
once, TK-113; the Claim Adjuster revert, TK-116), and the TK cases in
`experiments/tk/README.md`. Bomb, colonizer and defense values come from
the component table (`docs/COMPONENTS-STATUS.md`). Nothing else was used.

## Where it runs

`GenerateTurn` follows TAKEOVER.md "Where each task happens":

1. Before movement: each fleet's waypoint-0 task in fleet order (unloads,
   colonize), then the queued drops.
2. Movement, production and growth. A planet whose population dies out is
   emptied.
3. The after-movement phase records ownership, then battles, then bombing
   in fleet order, then the waypoint-0 tasks of the fleets' new locations,
   then the drops, then the second research level-up check.

A fleet's current task is `Fleet.Task`. A fleet that moves leaves it and
takes up `Waypoint.Task` of the waypoint it arrives at.

## Implementation assumptions

Elegy's own choices where TAKEOVER.md is silent. They are **not**
established Stars! behavior; each is marked `ASSUMPTION Tn` in the code.

| Id | What Elegy does | Why |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Away from a planet and any salvage, a fleet that shares its position with another fleet keeps the cargo it would unload (the action still clears). Anywhere else is deep space. | TAKEOVER.md defines deep space by the waypoint's target, which Elegy's task does not record, and unloads to fleets are not modelled. |
| T5 | A fleet whose waypoint 0 names a mineral packet or salvage (`Fleet.Target0`) loads from that object only, found by owner and number, in both load passes, even over a planet (CONFIRMED by prediction before movement, NS-1 A3–A6; MEASURED on arrival at salvage, NS-1 A1, A2), and from a named packet wherever it is (CONFIRMED by prediction before movement for 10, 40 and 150 ly, NS-1 B1, B2, NS-2 N, F; larger distances NOT RUN; after movement at a distance UNRESOLVED and possibly unreachable from legal orders, since the waypoint follows the packet, which reached it or fell short in NS-2 P1 and P1-F; Elegy loads at any distance); a named object that is gone, or any named object without space objects, is no source. Every other transport task (a plain-position or planet waypoint 0) loads from a planet first, else, away from a planet, from the first salvage object (object order) at the fleet's position and never from a packet. Unloads away from a planet go into the first salvage there, else as in deep space. | The original acts on the waypoint's target, looked up by owner and number (ORDERS.md "Salvage and mineral packet targets", "Arrival", BINARY-ONLY). OBJECTS.md "Salvage", "Loading" names a fleet at the salvage's position. A plain position or planet waypoint sharing a packet's position is not covered. ORDERS.md does not say what an unload at a packet does. NS-1: stars-elegy a1b3651, PARITY.md "A transport task whose waypoint names a packet or salvage (NS-1)", ORDERS.md "Clarified 2026-10-13". NS-2: stars-elegy f5bffd8, PARITY.md "A named packet at other distances and after movement (NS-2)". |
| T4 | Cargo types load in order (ironium, boranium, germanium, colonists), each from the space the earlier ones left. | TAKEOVER.md gives each action's amount, not the order between cargo types. |
| T6 | For colonists, salvage or a packet is a source holding none: their load actions settle as at a planet with no population (load all, load exactly and fill to clear; wait for and set amount wait). | OBJECTS.md says colonists cannot be loaded from salvage, not what becomes of the action; KERNEL.md counts salvage as a source. |
| T7 | Minerals unloaded into salvage beyond its room stay aboard (the action clears); colonists unloaded there are refused, as in deep space. | OBJECTS.md says an unload is accepted only up to the stored size, not where the rest goes, and says nothing of colonists. |
| T8 | A "fill to" or "wait for" v% target is ⌊v·capacity/100⌋ of that cargo type, counting only that type's cargo aboard, not the whole hold. | TAKEOVER.md says "up to v% of capacity"; FO-01 loaded into empty holds, where per-type and whole-hold targets agree, and its 50% of 210 is exact. |
| T9 | "Wait for" is met when the fleet holds at least its target of that type; then it clears like any satisfied load. | TAKEOVER.md says the fleet "waits until met" without defining met; FO-01-I (100% with 100 of 210 available) stayed unmet. |
| T10 | A fleet that can steal cargo from planets (a Robber Baron Scanner, COMPONENTS.md steals_cargo "fleets_and_planets") at another player's planet loads nothing, and its loads there wait, after movement too, with no message, holding the fleet until it gets new orders. | KERNEL.md makes that planet a source for such a fleet; Elegy does not model stealing cargo. |
| T11 | "Set waypoint to" that loads keeps the action until satisfied (the source ≤ v), so a short load holds the fleet, as an unmet "set amount to" does (KERNEL.md "Which loads are unmet"); once either unloads it clears. Otherwise the load pass settles them wherever the fleet is, even where it loads nothing (another player's planet, deep space, colonists at salvage): satisfied clears, short waits. | KERNEL.md names "set amount to" among the unmet loads but not "set waypoint to"; every vector case was satisfied in one pass. |
| T12 | Away from a planet, "set waypoint to" takes A from the salvage object at the fleet's position (T5), and in deep space A is 0, so the shortfall is unloaded and destroyed. | TAKEOVER.md defines A only for a planet. |
| T13 | After movement, of a task's loads with no usable source, the first in cargo order that wants more is the one refused and told; the cancel ends the whole task, so no later cargo type is told. | KERNEL.md and MESSAGES.md say the task is cancelled with a message naming a mineral, not which cargo type's. |
| T14 | Waypoint 0's named packet or salvage (`Fleet.Target0`) is set when the fleet arrives at a waypoint naming one, or by a waypoint order that replaces waypoint 0, and is kept across years (in the save) until the fleet sets off, a waypoint order replaces it, or the transport task ends; a task still waiting keeps it. | NS-1 found waypoint 0 aimed at the planet at the fleet's position at the end of every case, each of which ended its task (MEASURED); when the change happens, and a waiting task across years, are NOT RUN. |
| T15 | The design check (`checkDesigns`) runs in KERNEL.md step 1a.2, the ship-design housekeeping, after the orders and before the waypoint check, with the tech from before the year's research. Starbase designs are not checked. A part restricted only by lesser racial traits, or that some primary racial trait may not build (PRTNot), is checked like an unrestricted part. Mass, fuel and cargo capacity lose what a removed part added (a misslotted part added none, and is removed with no other change). An emptied engine slot is filled with the basic engine (Quick Jump 5) at the slot's capacity, as `Catalog.ReadDesign` does. Fleets keep their fuel and cargo, even above the new capacities. A design given with a fleet transfer is checked against the recipient's tech. | TAKEOVER.md says the check happens when the year is generated and covers ship designs, not when in the year or what else changes; T-37 removed no engine, fuel or cargo part and no LRT-restricted part, and gave no design to another player. Smart and Peerless (PRTNot Inner Strength) were removed in the tech 3 run (MEASURED). The basic engine adds no fuel or cargo capacity. |

The scan for several drops starts at the first dropping player, so a lone
attacker of strength 0 (Alternate Reality colonists, `k = 0`) lands; the
spec does not give the scan's start value. The code says so at
`dropWinner`.

## Not modelled

- every fuel action of a transport task. "Load all", "load exactly",
  "fill to", "wait for", "set amount to", "set waypoint to", fleet
  transfers, remote
  mining, mine laying, cargo given to other players' fleets and colonists
  dropped by a manual cargo order are implemented (KERNEL-STATUS.md,
  ORDERS-LAYER-STATUS.md);
- the old owner's message beyond `EventPlanetEmptied`.
- stealing cargo (a Robber Baron's loads at another player's planet wait, T10), and loads from another player's fleet (0x120): transport tasks do not target fleets.
